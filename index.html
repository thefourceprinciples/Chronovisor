Epistemics & provenance

Every claim carries a burden.

Chronovisor separates direct attestation from inference, analogy, reconstruction, and unresolved possibility. Sources should support the claim actually attached to them—not merely the surrounding topic.

Publication rules

  • Similarity permits investigation; it does not establish transmission.
  • Recurrence is not independent corroboration when it inherits the original premise.
  • Contradictory evidence is recorded, not silently repaired.
  • Confidence can move downward as readily as upward.
  • Earlier sweeps may be reclassified when A0 fails to recover their architecture.
